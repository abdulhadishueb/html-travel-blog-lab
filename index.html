Adventures in Fire Island

Trip Summary

Last weekend I explored Fire Island, a peaceful stretch of beach with soft sand, rolling dunes, and amazing views of the lighthouse.

I spent the day walking along the boardwalk trails, watching seabirds, and enjoying the sunset over the Atlantic Ocean. It was the perfect escape from the busy city.
